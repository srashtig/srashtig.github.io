What SEIR taught us about pandemic policy

Jul 2025 · Code · Paper

My COVID-19 work extended the standard SEIR framework to account for asymptomatic transmission. We added a compartment for asymptomatic carriers and used the model to compare testing and quarantine strategies.

Model features

  • Compartments: Susceptible, Exposed, Asymptomatic, Symptomatic and Recovered.
  • Transmission from both symptomatic and asymptomatic people.
  • Environmental (fomite) transmission through a pathogen compartment.
  • Analytical solutions and simulations to compare interventions.

What we found

Identifying asymptomatic cases early flattens the infection peak substantially and shrinks the outbreak. The same statistical toolkit I use for gravitational-wave data carried over directly to forecasting epidemics.
