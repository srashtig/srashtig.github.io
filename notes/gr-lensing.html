Can lensed gravitational waves test Einstein's gravity?

Jul 2025 · Paper (polarizations) · Paper (birefringence)

When a galaxy or cluster lenses a gravitational wave, we receive several copies of the same signal. Those copies make a natural experiment for testing theories of gravity.

What lensed copies let us test

  • Polarization modes: general relativity predicts two; alternative theories allow more.
  • Birefringence: whether lensing delays the two polarizations differently.
  • Earth's rotation: delayed copies arrive at different detector orientations, which acts like extra detectors.

The method

We run Bayesian model comparison on lensed pairs: general relativity against extended polarization models, and birefringence against none. Even without a detection, these tests constrain new physics more tightly than solar-system tests or single-event gravitational-wave observations.
