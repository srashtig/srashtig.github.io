Research

Gravitational waves are a new window to the universe, first observed directly in 2015 by the LIGO-Virgo detectors. My research focuses on gravitational lensing of gravitational waves, with applications to cosmology, the large-scale structure of the universe and alternative theories of gravity. I work where mathematical and computational modeling meets data analysis and machine learning. Earlier, I also worked on COVID-19 disease modeling and nonlinear dynamics.
